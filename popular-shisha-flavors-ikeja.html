Double Apple (Two Apples)

Double Apple leans heavily into sweet anise and dark licorice notes, backed by subtle caramelized apple skins. It hits the back of the palate with an earthy, spicy richness. Because it uses robust tobacco leaves, it takes heavy heat without burning — the top pick for seasoned smokers who want a bold, traditional session.

Love 66

Love 66 opens with sweet honeydew melon and tangy passionfruit, instantly followed by a cooling menthol wash. The cooling note prevents the tropical fruit syrup from feeling cloying on warm Lagos evenings. It smokes best with a fluff pack.

Blue Mist

Blue Mist features a distinct candied blueberry note paired with subtle mint. It is exceptionally forgiving with charcoal management, making it hard to accidentally scorch. For people stepping into flavored tobacco for the first time, this is one of the safest crowd-pleasers.

Gum Mint

Gum Mint delivers a nostalgic, clean spearmint flavor that tastes like classic chewing gum. While delicious on its own as a palate cleanser, it also serves as the ultimate utility flavor — adding a 20% pinch of Gum Mint to any overly sweet fruit blend instantly brings balance.

Lady Killer

Lady Killer blends ripe stone fruits: juicy mango and soft peach, followed by hints of cantaloupe and soft red berries. There is just enough cooling to keep the dense peach syrup light on the tongue.

How to Pack and Mix Your Bowls

When packing blonde leaf blends like Love 66 or Lady Killer, use the fluff pack method:

  1. Separate the sticky tobacco leaves on a tray.
  2. Sprinkle the leaves lightly into your bowl until they reach just below the rim.
  3. Do not compress the tobacco — open air pockets let hot air circulate evenly.
  4. Poke clean, spaced holes through your foil.

Key Rule of Heat Management: Shisha tobacco should never touch your aluminum foil directly. Leave a two-millimeter gap so the charcoal heat bakes the molasses rather than chars the leaves.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which shisha flavor is easiest for beginners?

Blue Mist is the most beginner-friendly flavor because its sweet blueberry and soft mint notes do not turn bitter if the bowl gets slightly too hot.

Can I mix Double Apple with mint flavors?

Yes. Pairing Double Apple with a small amount of Gum Mint (around 20%) cuts through the heavy licorice aroma and softens the throat hit.

How do I store opened tubs of shisha in Lagos?

Store opened shisha in an airtight container inside a dark cupboard at room temperature. Never keep it in the refrigerator — condensation separates the glycerin from the tobacco leaves.
